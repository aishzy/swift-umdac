### Notes on individual checks

- **`first_payment_recovered = 3`.** Three rows share epoch `1685363344`; the first
  payment is therefore present rather than lost to the embedded disclaimer.
- **`path_gap_formula_max_deviation_pp = 0.0949`.** The observed rate of at least
  one hidden hop matches `1 − 0.95^(participants−1)` within 0.095 percentage
  points for 2–6 participants. A tolerance of 0.2 pp is used because the residual
  is ordinary sampling/discretisation noise, not a broken rule.
- **IBAN checks.** For 28-character `_FAKE_` accounts, the embedded country and
  4-digit bank code are compared with the BIC. There are 5,442,631 ordering and
  5,405,035 beneficiary **rows** with IBAN-style accounts; both mismatch counts
  are 0.
- **`accounts_with_multiple_types = 0`.** Every ordering account is associated with
  exactly one ordering party type (`B` or `C`), which is a generator invariant.
- **Timezone checks.** `country_tz_coverage = 228` confirms the ISO2 → IANA map
  covers every ordering country, and `local_hour_rows_total = 10,000,000` confirms
  every payment joins to a timezone and lands in exactly one local hour.

### Reading the comparison

- **Structural rates are very close.** Routing mix, path-gap rates, endpoint match
  rates, local-currency share and anomaly rate all sit within about one percentage
  point of the full data.
- **Heavy tails move the medians.** `median_usd` is 8.3% lower in the sample and
  `median_bc_usd` 7.3% lower, because a 5% account sample can include or exclude a
  few very high-volume senders. Percentile-based aggregates are the stable choice
  for row-level analysis; the committed aggregates always use the full data.
- **Coverage.** All 228 ordering countries are present. The sample covers 220 of
  229 beneficiary countries and 152 of 155 currencies. The B-B and B-C shares are
  within 1 pp of the full data; C-B and C-C are 0.35 pp and 1.08 pp higher.
- **`covered_pct` (ratio 0.82).** `Covered` payments are only 4.2% of rows and are
  concentrated in a small number of accounts; a 5% account draw is noisy for this
  category.

## 3. Reproduce

```bash
duckdb -f scripts/prepare_data.sql
duckdb -f scripts/verify_data.sql
```

The second command rewrites `aggregates/qa_checks.csv` and
`aggregates/qa_sample_vs_full.csv`. All checks must print `PASS`; any `FAIL` means
an input or transformation changed.
